Cassette Motion Pro v0.10.6 - Report Preview Review

This update makes reports easier to review before saving or sending a client PDF.

What changed:

- Adds a Preview Report button to the Bike Fit Workspace.
- Preview Report saves the session, generates the report, and opens it
  immediately.
- Adds a Review before sending checklist at the top of generated HTML reports.
- The review checklist is hidden when printing or saving the report as PDF.
- Keeps the v0.10.5 Fit Summary workflow.
- Keeps the v0.10.4 installer resource hardening.
- Updates app/build/installer/report versions to 0.10.6.

Upload steps:

1. Open your GitHub repository:
   https://github.com/spooled/cassette-motion-pro

2. Upload everything from this package to the repo root.

   Important:
   - Include the hidden .github folder.
   - The workflow file must end up here:

     .github/workflows/build.yml

3. Commit with this message:

   Add v0.10.6 report preview review

4. Go to Actions.

5. Open the newest run.

6. Download:

   CassetteMotionPro-Windows-0.10.6

What to test:

1. Open Bike Fit Workspace.
2. Open or create a fit session.
3. Click Preview Report.
4. Confirm the report opens in your browser.
5. Confirm the top of the report shows Review before sending.
6. Click Print / Save PDF.
7. Confirm the review checklist does not appear in the print/PDF preview.
8. Open INSTALLER-BUILD-STATUS.txt from the Windows bundle.
9. Confirm it says Installer build status: SUCCESS.
10. Confirm the bundle contains CassetteMotionPro-Installer-0.10.6.exe.
11. Run the installer on Windows.
12. Confirm the app opens after install.
